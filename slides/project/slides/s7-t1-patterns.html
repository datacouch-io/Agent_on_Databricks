The point of this slide is that these are not four technologies. They are four answers to one question: who holds the plan? Manager-workers: the manager holds it, implicitly, one decision at a time. Planner-executor: the plan is written down before anything runs, which is the only pattern here where a human can approve it. Hierarchical: the plan is split across levels. Swarm: nobody holds it. Walk the diagrams, not the prose. Each one is the same mechanism from the previous slide - a worker exposed as a tool, a model choosing - rearranged. The one to dwell on is planner-executor, because it is the pattern people have not usually considered and it is the one that makes a multi-step agent auditable. A written plan is a thing you can diff between runs, which is what Session 6 will ask of everything else. Expected question: "Which does LangGraph give me?" All of them - it is a graph runtime, not a pattern. That is the Session 2 argument again: the framework supplies the plumbing, you still choose the shape. Timing: 5 minutes.
