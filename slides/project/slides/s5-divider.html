Tools and Governance

Goal: make a tool a governed object, then watch two identities see two different agents.

2 hours  |  Labs 5A, 5B

Session 1 made the case that a rule in code beats a rule in a prompt. Session 5 goes one step further: a rule in the permission system beats a rule in your code, because it holds for callers you did not write. The demonstration is two service principals, one agent and one tool. Neither identity is told what it may do. The platform decides, and the agent's own capabilities change underneath it. Timing: 1 minute.
