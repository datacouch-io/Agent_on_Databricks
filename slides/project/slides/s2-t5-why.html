Why framework choice matters less than seam discipline

What goes wrong when the framework is the architecture

Teams adopt a framework and let its abstractions become their design. Then the framework changes — and this ecosystem moves fast — or they need something it does not express, and the migration is a rewrite because there was no boundary between their logic and its idioms.

The engineering value of the seam

With core.py behind a fixed interface, a framework is something you adopt inside a component rather than something you build your product on.

Frameworks are good at

Boilerplate, adapters, graph state, checkpointing, streaming plumbing.

Frameworks are bad at

Being the thing you cannot leave. Every one of them is younger than your data.

The test

Could you replace it in a sprint? If not, it is your architecture, not your dependency.

Real scenario

This course was built across a change of execution environment, a change of retriever, and the arrival of a managed assistant. core.py did not change. That is not because we chose well — it is because nothing vendor-specific was ever allowed inside it.

Adopt frameworks freely. Just keep them on one side of a seam you own.
