Run this live after the Knowledge Store additions, so the room sees one to three in the same sitting. Before you run it, ask people to predict which of the three cases fails. Most will get the revenue one and miss the top-five, because the test-account problem is invisible unless you know the data. The "orders by region" case passing from the start is deliberate and useful. It shows the suite is not simply failing everything — Genie is competent at the questions that need no business knowledge, and that is the honest baseline. The right-hand panel is the summary. No model change, no prompt engineering, no deployment. Two sentences of curation moved one of three to three of three. That is a very unusual effort-to-outcome ratio in this field and it is worth naming as such. Then set the expectation for real use: your first suite will be mostly failures, and that is the point. The failures are a prioritised list of the definitions your organisation has never written down. Likely question: "Can this run in CI?" Through the API, yes, and that is the right home for it. Treat the space's configuration as code and the suite as its test run. Timing: 10 minutes.
