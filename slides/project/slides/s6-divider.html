Evaluation, Optimization and Deployment

Goal: measure the agent, distrust the measurement, then put it behind an endpoint.

2 hours  |  Labs 6A, 6B

Session 6 is where this course stops being about building and starts being about evidence. It is also the session with the most uncomfortable content, because the headline finding is that three separate automated judges in this course were wrong — penalising correct refusals, failing correct answers against rules the question never invoked. You are going to learn to read a rationale before you believe a score. Timing: 1 minute.
