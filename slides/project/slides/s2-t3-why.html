Why RAG alone answers only half the question

What goes wrong with RAG only

“Can I return the headphones I bought last month?” needs two facts: what the customer actually bought and when, and what the policy permits. A document retriever has the second and guesses at the first — and a guessed purchase date produces a confident, wrong answer.

Why the model should choose, not you

You cannot know in advance which questions need the table, which need the documents, and which need both in sequence. That is exactly the condition from Session 1 that justifies an agent.

Policy only

“Our policy allows returns within 30 days.” True, and useless to this customer.

Order only

“You bought headphones on 28 September.” True, and does not answer the question.

Both, in order

“You bought them on 28 September, so you are within the 30-day window.”

Notice the third answer required the order lookup first — because the policy question cannot be resolved without the date. The sequence was decided at runtime, by the model.

38

This is where Session 1's abstract criterion becomes a concrete design. Work the three panels as a story. Policy alone gives a true statement that helps nobody. Order alone gives a true statement that does not answer. The useful answer needs both, and — this is the part to emphasise — it needs them in an order that depends on the data: you cannot evaluate the thirty-day rule until you know the purchase date. That is the runtime control flow from Session 1's decision test, appearing in the most mundane possible customer-service question. It is worth saying explicitly: this is why the refund scenario was classified as an agent, and now you can see the mechanism. The business framing: a support agent that only does RAG will quote policy accurately at customers whose specific situation it has not checked. That is worse than unhelpful — it is authoritative and wrong, and it generates the complaint rather than resolving it. Likely question: "Could I just always fetch the order first, then search policy?" Then you have written a workflow, and for a narrow product that may be the right call. It breaks the moment a question needs two policy lookups, or none. Timing: 3 minutes.
