Where they agree

Where they diverge

Ask about “a laptop that arrived smashed”. Keyword search has no entry for smashed. Vector search finds the damaged-goods clause.

That difference is the whole argument for embeddings — and you can now state it as a result from your own run, not as a vendor claim.
