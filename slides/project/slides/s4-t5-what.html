What is Unity Catalog semantics?

Unity Catalog semantics is the set of tools for defining standard business metrics, terms and organisational structure on top of your catalog data — so people and AI read the data the same way.

Metric views are defined over source tables, views and queries, and consumed from both code and no-code interfaces.
Source: docs.databricks.com/aws/en/uc-semantics/metric-views/

Metric views

Reusable SQL objects that govern a KPI. Securable Unity Catalog objects with ordinary privileges.

Domains

An organisational layer grouping assets by business purpose, browsable from Discover.

Pages

Governed documents defining business concepts, as an authoritative source for people and Genie One.

Certification

Signals marking an asset trusted — or deprecated. Steers Genie toward endorsed resources.

This is the modelled half of the Genie Ontology: context you define, govern and certify deliberately — as opposed to context the platform infers from your assets.
