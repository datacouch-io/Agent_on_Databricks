"Nightly revenue summary" sounds like something a model should write, and it is — but writing the sentence is a step, not a decision. The schedule is fixed, the query is fixed, the recipient list is fixed. The model is a formatter. Scenario four is worth drawing out because it shows the criterion working positively. You read the error log, and what it says determines whether you next look at the deploy history, the database metrics or the upstream service. You cannot enumerate those branches in advance, and that is precisely what the agent loop buys you. Likely question: "Can something be half and half?" Yes, and it is usually the right answer in production: a workflow with one agentic step inside it, scoped to the part that genuinely needs runtime decisions. That shape is the most under-used design in this field. Timing: 25 minutes including discussion.
