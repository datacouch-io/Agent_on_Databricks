What is the Genie family?

Genie is the Databricks AI experience for users. Three products share one governed foundation.

Genie One

The interface business users see. Ask data questions in natural language, browse assets, open dashboards, run Databricks Apps — one place, every answer grounded in your data.

Genie Agents

The domain-specific environments data teams configure: trusted datasets, sample queries, metrics and business rules. These power the answers Genie One gives.

Genie Code

The AI coding and data assistant for developers, inside the workspace — notebooks, the SQL editor, jobs, dashboards and pipelines.

Start Session 4 by separating three things that get called "Genie" interchangeably, because the room will otherwise spend the session unsure which one you mean. Genie One is what a business user opens. Genie Agents are what your data team builds and governs — the datasets, the metrics, the business rules. Genie Code is what the engineers in this room will use daily inside the workspace. Same governed foundation, three audiences. The ownership point is worth making early: Genie Agents are a data governance artefact that happens to be consumed by a model. The people who own metric definitions should own them, not the ML team.
