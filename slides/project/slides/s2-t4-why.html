Why authentication is the hardest thing to port

What goes wrong

Retrieval ports cleanly, model calls port cleanly, and then identity does not. A notebook runs as you. A job runs as a service principal. A serving endpoint runs as itself and must have declared its resources at log time. Each has a different failure, and the failure usually appears far from its cause.

The engineering value of naming it early

Once identity is an input rather than an assumption, moving the agent between runtimes stops being a migration and becomes a configuration change.

Notebook

Your identity. Everything you can see, the agent can see. Flattering and misleading.

Job

A service principal with its own grants. The first place least privilege becomes real.

Endpoint

Its own identity, with resources declared at log time. Omit one and it fails at runtime.

Real scenario

An agent works perfectly in a notebook and fails on its first real question after deployment, with a permission error on the index. Nothing about the agent changed — the notebook had been running as a person who could read everything.

Know the portability limits too: managed retrieval, governed tools and traces are platform features. The agent logic ports; the governance does not, and that is a fair trade to make knowingly.
