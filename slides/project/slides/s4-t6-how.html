How a question resolves against the ontology

1 · Question

from a person, or from your agent

2 · Retrieve

candidate snippets, gated by your permissions

3 · Rank

by authority: source, usage, freshness

4 · Resolve

conflicts between snippets that disagree

5 · Answer

using only sources you are permitted to see

Step 2 is the security boundary

Snippets are gated by Unity Catalog permissions on the assets they came from. Nothing you cannot see can reach your answer.

Step 3 is the quality lever

Authority rises with provenance, usage and freshness. A certified metric view used daily outranks a stale query from last year.

Step 4 is what you cannot do by hand

Organisations hold contradictory definitions. Resolving them per question, per user, is the work the layer exists to do.

Your two levers

Certify what is authoritative and deprecate what is not. Those signals move snippets up and down the ranking, which is how you steer answers without rewriting prompts.

Why latency improves too

Ranked, relevant context is smaller context — the Session 1 point, applied by the platform.
