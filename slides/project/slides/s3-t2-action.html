Notebook gotcha

Cell 0 must be %pip install -q openai followed by dbutils.library.restartPython(). Serverless has no OpenAI client pre-installed, and the install needs a restart to take effect.

You may also see a Pydantic serializer warning on first call. It is harmless and noisy; the labs suppress it with a filtered warning and a comment explaining why, rather than hiding it silently.

Six lines, and they are the same six lines in every notebook in the course. Worth putting on screen once so nobody wonders where the client came from later. The WorkspaceClient is doing one job here: handing you the host and a token so you do not paste credentials into a notebook. In a job or a serving endpoint it resolves those from the environment automatically. Read the white panel aloud, because it closes the loop on Session 2. Two Databricks-specific lines exist, and they are in the notebook that constructs the client, not in the agent. core.py receives a configured client and a retriever and knows nothing about either.
