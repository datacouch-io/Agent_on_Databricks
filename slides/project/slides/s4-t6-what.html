What is the Genie Ontology?

The Genie Ontology is the unified context layer that gives the Genie family a business-aware map of your organisation. It brings together two kinds of context.

1 — Unity Catalog semantics (modelled)

Context you define, govern and certify: metric views, domains and Pages. Deliberate, owned and reviewable.

2 — Inferred context (snippets)

Context the platform extracts and maintains automatically from your existing assets — metric views, dashboards, SQL queries and Genie Agents. Metric definitions, authoritative sources, business rules.

Snippet

One unit of inferred context, extracted from an asset you already have.

Authority score

Based on where it came from, how often it is used, and how fresh it is.

Permission-gated

Genie only uses snippets from assets you are permitted to see.

Shared

Genie One and Genie Code search the same knowledge base, so curation applies across both.

This is the advanced topic the outline flags, and it is the most architecturally interesting thing in Session 4. The structure is the content: two kinds of context in one layer. The modelled half is the previous slide — metric views, domains and Pages, which you write and certify. The inferred half is new and worth dwelling on: the platform reads assets you already have — dashboards, SQL query history, metric views, Genie Agents — and extracts snippets of context from them automatically. That is a different proposition from "configure your AI". It means the curation work your organisation has already done, in artefacts built for other purposes, becomes context for every Genie experience. Authority scoring is how it stays sane. Each snippet carries a score based on provenance, usage frequency and freshness, so a definition from a certified metric view used daily outranks one inferred from a query somebody ran once last year. When snippets conflict, Genie ranks and resolves. And permissions are enforced at retrieval: Genie only uses snippets from assets you can see. Two people asking the same question can correctly get different answers. Likely question: "Can I see what snippets exist?" They are surfaced in the Genie experiences and derived from assets you own. Treat the modelled layer as your lever — that is the half you control directly. Timing: 4 minutes.
