What is structured versus unstructured retrieval?

Two different ways of fetching context, with different guarantees. Real questions need both, and the model should choose.

Structured — lookup by key

A table, a key, one row. get_order_status('ORD-1042') returns the order or nothing. Exact, cheap, no embedding, no ranking, no ambiguity.

Unstructured — search by meaning

Prose, a query, the nearest passages. Approximate, ranked, citable. Finds things that share no words with your question.

Guarantee

Structured: correct or absent.
Unstructured: plausible, with a score.

Failure mode

Structured: not found.
Unstructured: confidently irrelevant.

Cost

Structured: a query.
Unstructured: an embedding plus a search.

The analogy

Structured retrieval is looking someone up in the phone book. Unstructured retrieval is asking a librarian who read the whole collection. You would not use the librarian to find a phone number, and you would not use the phone book to find out what the policy says about damaged goods.

A short definitional slide, but it corrects a real and costly habit: treating "agent on enterprise data" as a synonym for RAG. The distinction that matters is the guarantee. A key lookup is right or it returns nothing. A similarity search always returns something — the nearest thing it has — and "nearest" can be very far away. Those are different trust levels and they want different handling in your prompt and your evaluation. Say the failure modes aloud. Structured retrieval fails loudly and usefully: no such order. Unstructured retrieval fails quietly and plausibly: three passages that look relevant and are not. The second is far more dangerous in an agent, because the model has no way to tell a good match from a bad one once the text is in its context. The phone book and librarian analogy does the work here. Nobody asks a librarian for a phone number. Likely question: "Could I not put the orders in the vector index too?" You could, and you would turn an exact lookup into a fuzzy one. Embedding an order id gets you orders with similar ids. Keep exact things exact. Timing: 3 minutes.
