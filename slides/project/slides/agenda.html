Note the proportions: four hours before Databricks is mentioned, ten hours after. That is deliberate. If we started with Databricks you could not tell which ideas are agent engineering and which are product features. By Session 3 you will have built an agent twice — once in plain Python, once behind interfaces — so when AI Search and MLflow appear you recognise what they are replacing. Expected question: "Can I skip to Session 3 if I already know agents?" You can follow it, but Sessions 1 and 2 establish the three ideas the rest of the course keeps returning to: stopping conditions, controls in code, and where a rule lives. Timing: 2 minutes.
